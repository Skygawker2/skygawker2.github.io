Short vertical clips, straight to your TikTok drafts

Skygawker Clip Uploader is a small tool, operated through a Telegram bot, that turns long videos and generated stories into short vertical (9:16) clips with subtitles.

How it works

  1. You send a video (or ask for a story) in the Telegram bot.
  2. The bot cuts and renders short vertical clips with captions.
  3. You connect your TikTok account once with Login with TikTok.
  4. Finished clips are uploaded to your TikTok inbox as drafts. You review, edit and publish them yourself in the TikTok app — nothing is posted publicly without you.

TikTok permissions used

  • user.info.basic — to show which TikTok account is connected (display name and avatar).
  • video.upload — to send finished clips to your TikTok drafts.

You can disconnect at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.
