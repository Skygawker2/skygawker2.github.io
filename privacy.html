Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what data Skygawker Clip Uploader (“the Service”) handles and why.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar — used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok — used only to upload clips to your drafts (video.upload).

We do not read your TikTok videos, messages, followers or analytics, and we never post publicly on your behalf.

Other data

The Telegram bot stores your Telegram user ID, your settings and the clips it generates, so it can deliver them to you.

How data is stored and shared

Data is stored on the operator's private server and used only to provide the Service. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, except that clips are sent to TikTok when you request an upload, and video text may be processed by an AI provider (Google Gemini) to select clip moments.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services); the tokens then stop working and are deleted from our server. Generated clips are deleted periodically. You can request deletion of all your data via the contact below.

Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.
